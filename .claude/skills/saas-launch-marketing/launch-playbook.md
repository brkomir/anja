# Launch Playbook

Week-by-week actions for a SaaS launch. Adapt timelines to your situation — the sequence matters more than the exact dates.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 8-6)

### Week 8: Positioning & Research

**Actions:**
- Write your positioning statement (use the framework in SKILL.md)
- Identify 3-5 communities where your target audience is active
- Research competitors: what do they say? What do users complain about?
- Start a "swipe file" of marketing copy you admire from similar products

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Positioning statement (one sentence)
- [ ] Community list with links and notes on culture/rules
- [ ] Competitor messaging analysis (what they say, what's missing)

### Week 7: Start Building in Public

**Actions:**
- Begin posting 3-5x/week about the problem domain (not your product yet)
- Join communities and start contributing genuinely
- Share interesting insights, opinions, or data about the space
- Start collecting "voice of customer" language from forums, reviews, comments

**Post Types That Work:**
- "I noticed [interesting pattern] about [domain]..."
- "Unpopular opinion: [contrarian take about the industry]"
- "I spent [time] analyzing [thing] — here's what I found"
- Behind-the-scenes build decisions: "We chose X over Y because..."
- Screenshots or short videos of work-in-progress (people love seeing things being built)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] First 5 posts published
- [ ] Joined and introduced yourself in 3+ communities
- [ ] Voice-of-customer swipe file started (10+ phrases/quotes)

### Week 6: Landing Page & Waitlist

**Actions:**
- Build a minimal waitlist landing page
- Set up email capture (ConvertKit, Resend + custom, Beehiiv, etc.)
- Share the waitlist with your growing audience
- Start DMing people who engage with your posts: "Would you want to try this?"

**Waitlist Landing Page Structure:**
1. Headline: the core value prop
2. Subheadline: one sentence expanding on how
3. Email input + CTA button
4. 3 bullet points of what they'll get
5. Optional: "Join [N] others on the waitlist"

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Waitlist page live
- [ ] Email capture working
- [ ] First 20-50 signups

## Phase 2: Asset Prep (Weeks 5-4)

### Week 5: Marketing Copy & Content

**Actions:**
- Write the full landing page copy (use section structure from SKILL.md)
- Draft Product Hunt listing: tagline (60 chars), description, topics
- Write your maker comment (the most-read content on your PH page)
- Create 10-15 social posts for launch week (pre-write, don't improvise)

**Email Sequence to Draft:**
1. **Welcome email** (when they join waitlist): "You're in. Here's what's coming..."
2. **Teaser email** (1 week before launch): "We launch next [day]. Here's a sneak peek..."
3. **Launch email** (launch day): "It's live. Here's your link..."
4. **48h follow-up**: "200 people tried it yesterday. Here's what they said..."
5. **One-week check-in**: "How's it going? Reply and tell me..."

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Full landing page copy drafted
- [ ] Product Hunt listing copy ready
- [ ] 10+ social posts pre-written
- [ ] 5-email launch sequence drafted

### Week 4: Visual Assets & Technical Prep

**Actions:**
- Capture real product screenshots at key moments (not mockups)
- Record a 60-90 second demo video (Loom is fine — authenticity > polish)
- Create OG images (1200x630) for all public pages
- Set up analytics, UTM parameters, and conversion tracking
- Add OpenGraph and Twitter Card metadata to all public pages
- Test social sharing previews (use opengraph.xyz or similar)

**Product Hunt Gallery (5-6 images):**
1. Hero shot: product in action, showing the core value
2. Problem → solution: before/after or "old way vs new way"
3. Key feature deep-dive
4. Another key feature or use case
5. Social proof: testimonial, user quote, or metric
6. Optional: pricing or "how it works" summary

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] 5+ product screenshots
- [ ] Demo video recorded and hosted
- [ ] OG images created and deployed
- [ ] Analytics live
- [ ] Social sharing previews working correctly

## Phase 3: Beta & Validation (Weeks 3-2)

### Week 3: Recruit & Run Beta

**Actions:**
- Invite 50-150 people from waitlist for early access
- Send personal invites (not bulk): "I'd love your feedback specifically because..."
- Schedule 15-minute calls to watch them use the product
- Create a feedback channel (Discord, Slack, or simple email thread)

**What to Watch For:**
- Where do they hesitate or get confused?
- What do they say "wow" or "cool" to?
- What feature do they ask about that doesn't exist?
- Do they understand the value prop within 60 seconds?
- Would they recommend this to a friend? (Ask directly)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] 50+ beta testers invited
- [ ] 10+ feedback calls completed
- [ ] Top 5 friction points documented
- [ ] 3+ testimonials collected (with permission to share)

### Week 2: Fix, Polish, Prepare

**Actions:**
- Fix critical issues from beta feedback
- Polish onboarding based on observed confusion points
- Finalize all marketing copy with insights from beta
- Confirm Product Hunt hunter and schedule launch date
- Brief your launch-day supporters with specific asks

**Supporter Briefing Template:**
```
Hey [name] — I'm launching [product] on [date].

Here's what would be amazing:
1. Check out the Product Hunt page on [day] morning
2. If you like it, leave a genuine comment about your experience
3. Share it with anyone you think would find it useful

Here's the link: [Product Hunt URL]

No pressure at all — only if you genuinely think it's worth sharing.
```

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Critical bugs fixed
- [ ] Onboarding polished
- [ ] 20+ supporters briefed
- [ ] Launch date confirmed

## Phase 4: Launch Week

### Day Before Launch

- [ ] Test signup → first value flow one final time
- [ ] Ensure billing works end-to-end
- [ ] Queue social posts with final timing
- [ ] Load email sequence into your email tool
- [ ] Have Product Hunt maker comment ready to paste
- [ ] Set an alarm for 11:50 PM PT (if PH launching at midnight)

### Launch Day

**Hour 0 (12:01 AM PT):** Product Hunt goes live. Post maker comment immediately.

**Hours 0-2:** Respond to every PH comment. Send launch email to waitlist. Post first X/Twitter thread.

**Hours 3-6:** Continue PH engagement. Post to Reddit (r/SaaS, r/startups, niche subs). Post on LinkedIn if relevant. Monitor for issues.

**Hours 6-12:** Continue engaging. Post second round of social content. DM supporters who haven't seen it yet. Go live on X Spaces if you have the audience for it.

**Hours 12-24:** Wind-down PH engagement. Share early results and reactions. Thank supporters publicly. Fix any issues that surfaced.

### Days 2-3: Follow-Up

- Send 48h follow-up email to waitlist
- Share launch metrics publicly: "Here's how day 1 went..."
- Respond to every piece of feedback
- Post a "lessons learned" thread

### Days 4-7: Ride the Wave

- Publish launch retrospective blog post
- Share user testimonials and reactions
- Post "here's what we're building next based on your feedback"
- Start converting free users to paid (if freemium)

## Phase 5: Post-Launch Growth (Weeks 2-8)

### Content Engine (Ongoing)

Publish 1-2 pieces/week. Types:

| Content Type | Purpose | Example |
|-------------|---------|---------|
| Problem-focused | SEO + awareness | "Why most photographers over-edit (and how to know when to stop)" |
| Tutorial/How-to | SEO + trust | "How to read a histogram for better photo editing decisions" |
| Behind-the-scenes | Community + trust | "How we built the AI critique engine" |
| User spotlight | Social proof | "How [user] improved their portfolio score from B to A" |
| Comparison | SEO + conversion | "[Product] vs the free alternative" |
| Data/insights | Virality | "We analyzed 10,000 photo critiques — here's what separates good from great" |

### Growth Channels (Prioritized)

1. **SEO content** — long-tail keywords around the problem domain
2. **Community participation** — ongoing, genuine, not promotional
3. **Partnerships** — complementary tools, photography educators, YouTube creators
4. **Newsletter** — weekly tips, product updates, user stories
5. **Social media** — continue build-in-public cadence
6. **Paid ads** — only after organic messaging is validated (usually month 2-3)

### Retention Focus

Acquisition without retention is a leaky bucket. Prioritize:

- Onboarding completion rate (is the first experience great?)
- Time to first "aha moment" (how fast do they get value?)
- Weekly active usage (are they coming back?)
- Feature adoption (are they discovering the depth of the product?)

## Photography-Niche Specific Tactics

For an AI photography product specifically:

**Where Photographers Hang Out:**
- Reddit: the general photography subreddits plus the dedicated critique and post-processing ones
- X/Twitter: photography communities, educator accounts
- YouTube: photography channels (potential partnership / review)
- Facebook groups: genre-specific photography groups (landscape, portrait, wildlife)
- Photography forums: the established enthusiast forums (gear, technique, film)
- Discord: photography servers

**Content That Resonates With Photographers:**
- Before/after editing comparisons
- "I processed this in Lightroom vs Capture One vs Darktable"
- Critique of well-known photographs (educational, not mean)
- Technical deep-dives (color theory, tonal hierarchy, etc.)
- Behind-the-scenes of the AI critique process

**Partnership Opportunities:**
- Photography educators (YouTube, courses)
- Camera/lens review channels
- Editing software communities
- Photography competition organizers
- Camera clubs and societies
