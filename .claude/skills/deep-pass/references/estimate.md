# `/deep-pass estimate` — in full (moved from `/wrap --estimate`; the deep-pass core carries a 1-line pointer under its 20,000 B ceiling)

**`/deep-pass estimate` — first asked for as `/wrap --estimate`, to size the deep pass a wrap needs and advise its model (Sonnet or Opus) and its depth.** A READ, not a pass: run `node scripts/deep-pass-estimate.mjs`, print its readout as reply text, and STOP — no step runs, nothing is written, the listener stays armed, and the summary is the script's own lines. It derives, from this session's transcript and the same strip function the pass slices by: the stripped size, the compaction and commit counts the 1b-DEEP trigger reads, the scan window (the covered-range record when a completed pass wrote one, else the whole transcript as a CEILING — the pass's own checkpoint walk may scope a first wrap smaller), the slice and wave counts at the digest default's 9 MB, the `/wrap --light` 10 MB and the `/wrap --full` 2 MB target, the per-slice token RANGES the measurement registry (not in this copy) holds for each model, and the five-hour share against the dispatch guard's threshold (read off the guard). Its advice is rule-bound and printed with its reason: the digest whenever the pass fires, the default (the light pass was the default before it), with the `/wrap --light` and `/wrap --full` slice and wave counts printed so each opt-in can be priced, Opus by default with Sonnet named as the alternative — the choice between the two is a preference (a fair paired read: 4 fair-effort slices of one session, scored unblinded by an Opus-family reader: Opus found more on 2, level on 2, Sonnet found things Opus missed on every slice and used slightly fewer tokens; a 2026-09-29 reading had 5 Opus slices at about twice the tokens of same-day Sonnet slices, but over a different window and with Sonnet at a lower effort (not like-for-like), so the cost reading is not settled), and "none until the reset" when the share is at or above the guard's line. The owner then types the wrap he wants (`/wrap`, `/wrap --light`, `/wrap --sonnet`, or a combination); the estimate never picks for him. `--self-test` pins the arithmetic; a unit test (not in this copy) runs it.

Why it lives here and not in `/wrap`: invoking a skill attaches its whole core, and re-attaches it after every compaction; the deep-pass core is under 20,000 B where the wrap core is ~45,400 characters. Being a `/deep-pass` invocation, it also leaves no `/wrap` record, so the checkpoint walk needs no exclusion for it (the 2026-10-29 `--estimate` exclusion is removed).
