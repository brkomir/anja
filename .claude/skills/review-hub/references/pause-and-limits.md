# Review Hub — the usage limit and the entry points' full text (moved, with the copy's edits, from `SKILL.md`; the core's `📁` stub points here and the core wins a disagreement; the ⏸ pause steps 1-11 went BACK into the core, since an entry point typed many times a month stays where the invocation loads it)

### Near the 5-hour limit — two rules (the light version, chosen over a script + warn hook)
1. **A lens stopped by the usage limit: first look for its full report at `.claude/state/review-reports/<sha>-<lens>.md` — the tool's BANKED sees only a hand-back, never that file; whole ⇒ read it and `mark --as dropped`. Otherwise run `/limit-recovery` and take its row — BANKED (collect the hand-back), RESUME, WAIT, or FRESH.** Warm or cold, at any size ⇒ resume; the exceptions are the tool's (a model change, a failed resume, a context that no longer fits, a death at the agent's own model's limit), so read the row and its reason off the tool, never from this paragraph. A resume runs on the model the lens's dispatch named, else its definition's pin, else the session's model (`limit-recovery/SKILL.md`; `scan` reads the dispatch's model from the agent's meta.json), so a lens dispatched on Fable resumes on Fable; one that died at Fable's own weekly limit is seated FRESH on Opus at once, and one on any other model that died at its own model's limit WAITs for that reset (`MODEL_LIMIT_MOVES` in `scripts/limit-recovery.py` is the one place it lives). The always-resume rule dropped an earlier room rule, which had itself replaced a rule that resumed a warm seat and fresh-seated a cold one carrying a large context (400k+). **Warm means its last model call is inside the SUB-AGENT cache window, which is 5 minutes here, not the session's hour** (sub-agents are a separate cache bucket; `subagentPromptCacheTtl` would set it to an hour; it is left off, on a measured ~16% higher sub-agent cache cost): measured 2026-10-11, 60 of 60 sub-agent transcripts from the previous 3 days (4 sessions) wrote cache at the 5-minute TTL only, while their parent sessions wrote at 1 hour — so a lens stopped by the 5-hour limit is cold by the reset in practice. RESUME, warm or cold ⇒ `SendMessage` to its agent id (on the scan row, or from `.claude/logs/subagent-dispatch.jsonl`, the start log; `agent-dispatch-memory.jsonl` carries no agent id) continues it with its pre-limit work intact; a cold one rewrites its cache once to get there. WAIT ⇒ nothing until that model's limit resets, then the row's reason says which (`limit-recovery/SKILL.md` step 3a). FRESH ⇒ a fresh seat over the tool's digest of the dead seat. Same round only: a new round after a committed fold is a fresh seat (`conventions.md`, the limit-reset row). *(Read "Resume a lens that hit the usage limit; never re-dispatch it" until 2026-10-11: it priced only the redo — four reviews redone from scratch, about 4-6M tokens — and never the cold re-cache of a resume. Priced 2026-10-12, the re-cache came out cheaper than the redo, so that rule's direction stood and only its "never" fell; the room line that first carried the exception was dropped the same day.)*
2. **Before seating a panel, read your own usage figure: `five_hour_pct` and `five_hour_resets_at` in `.claude/state/statusline-<sid8>.json`. Below 80%, the limit plays no part: seat the panel the change needs** (the hold at 90% or above is a separate rule, `agent-dispatch-session-limit-guard.py`, which denies new launches strictly above 90% on a reading at most 10 minutes old, unless the prompt carries `OWNER-ORDERED:`; at 97% or more of the all-models weekly limit the same guard holds the launch and has the seat ask the owner in its reply, without blocking). ⛔ **At no level is a panel shrunk, or one lens's questions folded into another seat, to fit the limit** (holding the request is better than a review done by halves). At 80% or above, if the file is under 15 minutes old (the keep-warm gate's `STATUS_FRESH_S`) and the limit will plausibly arrive before the panel could finish (median lens ~19 min; the rate is your sid8's `five_hour_pct` over the last ~15 lines of `.claude/logs/usage-meter.jsonl`, one a minute), hold the WHOLE request until the reset, seat none of it, and tell the owner. Say so in the ACK, subject `ACK <sha>: QUEUED, pos <n>/<N> — usage hold, starts at the 5-hour reset (~HH:MMZ)`: never the words HELD or paused, which mean the owner's pause, and a ledger row for it, if any, opens `QUEUED — usage hold` with no `fold-verify` in it. The requester's position is unchanged; only the start moves. The hold is the hub's own and lifts at the reset, unless `review-hub-paused-<sid8>` exists: the owner's pause outranks it. ⛔ **It is never `/review-hub --pause`**, which is the owner's and lifts only on his word (or, when he armed it with `--when usage>=N`, at the reset or on a switch to an account below N, and for the weekly line when the weekly figure falls under W: § ⏸ step 8). **A missing or stale file means UNKNOWN: seat normally.** The figure disagrees between sessions almost every time it was compared (177 of 179 minutes) and is empty 28% of the time. The disagreement is not several accounts at once: one account is in use at a time, and an idle session's figure stays where its last request left it, behind the sessions that are spending and, after a switch, still the previous account's (measured: concurrently ACTIVE sessions' weekly figures differed by more than 10 points in 0 of 9,373 minutes; their five-hour share was not compared, and nothing has measured why the figure is empty). That is why this stays a judgment rule, with no hook acting on it below 90%.

## Entry points — the full text (moved from the core; the table is in `references/entry-points.md`)

- **`/review-hub --pause`** · **`--resume`** — hold new reviews, panels and subagents; the running seats finish and their verdicts go out, everything else is ACKed as held. § ⏸ in the core. `--resume` lifts the pause only; it lists open deploy holds and withdraws just the ones on commits production does not read, and run with no pause marker it does that hold reading alone (§ ⏸ step 3).
- **`/review-hub --pause --when usage>=N`** — the same pause, entered by a watch when this session's five-hour usage reaches N% (or its weekly usage 97%), and lifted by a second watch at that window's reset, or sooner on a switch to an account below the line; a pause the owner types still waits for the owner's word. § ⏸ steps 6-10.
