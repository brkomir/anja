The notice the licence requires to travel with the files is `.claude/NOTICE`. Whoever prepares or reviews a copy and finds outside text with no entry here holds that file back until the entry exists; a passage that is derived from outside sources without being a copy carries a source line in the file itself instead, and the second section lists those.
